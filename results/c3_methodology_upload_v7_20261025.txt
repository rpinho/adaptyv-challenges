We combined two complementary de novo design approaches against the GLP1R extracellular domain (ECD), targeting the hydrophobic groove that natively accommodates GLP-1's C-terminal helix. These are ECD-groove binders; agonism is not structurally supported; no TMD engagement has been demonstrated. No antagonism is claimed as an experimental result.

Approach 1 - BindCraft (8 designs). We used BindCraft with forced targeting on the GLP1R ECD from PDB 6X18 (chain R). Hotspot residues were selected to cover the groove: W33, W39, R40, E68, Y69, W91 and surrounding positions (A67-A69, A88-A91, A121, A123, A128, A137-A145). The campaign explored binder lengths 45-100 residues over 3000 trajectories, yielding 10 ranked designs. Two were dropped: one for excessive steric clashes (13 all-atom clashes), one for marginal ESMFold refold confidence (pLDDT 68.4). The remaining 8 designs span iPTM 0.76-0.85 with AF2 pLDDT 0.83-0.93 and full contact across all 6 key groove residues.

Approach 2 - RFD4-Proteina plus ProteinMPNN (11 designs). We used NVIDIA's RFD4-Proteina diffusion model as an orthogonal generation method, targeting the same GLP1R ECD groove with hotspot conditioning on W33, W39, R40, E68, Y69, W91, Y88 and R121. The screening target was the exact 111-residue ECD used by the BindCraft complexes. 200 designs were generated in earlier rounds; for this round we generated 36 new independent backbones (12 each at lengths 52-64, 65-80 and 81-100), designed two ProteinMPNN sequences per backbone (v_48_020, sampling temperature 0.1, seed 20261028, binder chain A designed with the target chain held fixed), and applied the same soluble/non-TM sequence filters used throughout: canonical amino acids, maximum 19-residue Kyte-Doolittle window below 2.0, overall GRAVY below 0.4, and charged fraction (K/R/D/E/H) at least 0.18. 72 sequences were produced; 63 passed ESMFold monomer refold (mean pLDDT >= 80, >= 90% of residues at pLDDT >= 70, C-alpha RMSD <= 3.0 A against the designed backbone) and 56 of those also passed the sequence filters. Structural clusters were defined on the designed backbones (near-identical iff TM-score >= 0.65 AND alignment coverage >= 0.80); selection took 24 sequences across 24 clusters with at most one per cluster.

All 24 selected designs were then screened as complexes with the identical AlphaFold-Multimer v3 protocol used for the BindCraft and earlier RFD4 designs (three recycles, five models, same 111-residue target, no template supplied), on an A40 GPU whose port had been validated to reproduce the Modal reference scores exactly. A design was promotable only if it simultaneously satisfied: complex iPTM at or above the pre-registered 0.850 floor (the floor is max(0.76, the weakest BindCraft anchor iPTM) and was not lowered); ESMFold monomer refold eligibility as above; structural checks against the predicted complex (binder pLDDT >= 80, at least 5 groove residues contacted, at least 20 binder-target heavy-atom contacts within 5 A, zero heavy-atom clashes below 2.0 A, and no chain-integrity defect); the four sequence filters above; and a measured backbone-independence requirement. Independence was measured, never assumed: TM-score and coverage were computed for every candidate against every retained design, and a candidate was accepted only if it was not near-identical (TM >= 0.65 and coverage >= 0.80) to any other row in the final portfolio. A missing measurement is treated as a failure, not as evidence of independence.

Eleven RFD4/ProteinMPNN designs are in the final 20-row portfolio. Six of them (c3_glp1r_ecd_26, _188, _132, _14, _46, _127) were newly screened as complexes this round and replaced correlated point-mutant variants from the previous version; the other five (c3_glp1r_ecd_12, _29, _151, _171, _172) were already present from the earlier RFD4 round. One point-mutation variant (c3_v_l65_E5Q) is retained. No design was promoted on sequence novelty alone.

All 20 designs were independently validated using ESMFold (facebook/esmfold_v1) single-sequence structure prediction. Design lengths range from 52 to 100 residues (mean 75) with net charges from -11 to 0. The portfolio has a mean Trp+Tyr fraction of 6.4%, with 4 designs above 9% Trp+Tyr for aromatic groove complementarity. Independent biophysical review by dual AI agents screened for extreme charge, tandem repeats, aggregation-prone hydrophobicity, poly-amino-acid runs and transmembrane-like segments. Alternative designs were excluded for concrete measured failures - below-floor iPTM, ESMFold refold failure, steric clash, chain-integrity defects, or redundancy with a retained backbone - not for small confidence differences. Individual candidate scores and rejection reasons are listed in the accompanying per-candidate screen report.

Limitations: iPTM, pTM and pLDDT are model confidence metrics, not measured affinities (KD) or experimental binding evidence. All designs target the ECD groove; none has been shown to engage the transmembrane domain, and agonism is not demonstrated. Solubility and non-transmembrane character are sequence heuristics, not measurements.
