# C3 E3 diversity pilot — result (2026-10-25, ~23:02–00:10 ET)

**Status: complete, verified, NOT promoted, NOT submitted.** E3 is a separate research cohort;
it never enters the frozen E2 gate and it does not modify audited v8.

## Design
Astra-authorized bounded pilot: 72 RFD4 backbones = 3 hotspot arms × 3 length bins × 8 seeds,
unchanged `recycle_strategy=medium/front_loaded`, `num_replicates=1`, `batch_size=1`.
Target unchanged: 111-mer GLP1R ECD, sequence sha256 `5c2bae2d…36b3`.

| arm | hotspots (chain F) |
|---|---|
| ctrl | 5, 11, 12, 40, 41, 60, 63, 93 (round-2 set) |
| subA | 11, 12, 40, 60 |
| subB | 41, 60, 63, 93 |

## The main finding
Novelty and clash-freedom are **different levers**, and they pull in opposite directions here:

| arm | novel backbones | iPTM≥.85 | **eligible** | total clashes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ctrl | 6/24 (25%) | 7 | **3** | 11 |
| **subA** | 12/24 (50%) | 6 | **4** | **3** |
| subB | 13/24 (54%) | 6 | **0** | **16** |

- **subA ({11,12,40,60}) is the best arm**: fewest clashes by far (3 across 6 designs) while still
  roughly doubling novelty vs ctrl.
- **subB is the trap**: the most novel backbones (54%) but **zero** eligible designs and 16 clashes.
  Buying new folds by moving hotspots to {41,60,63,93} bought clashing interfaces with them.
- Higher iPTM did **not** buy eligibility: all 23 scored designs were ≥0.81 iPTM and 19 were
  ≥0.85, yet only 7 are clash-free. The binder–target interface geometry, not the confidence
  score, is the binding constraint — consistent with round 2.

## What this does NOT establish
- It does not authorize a v9. Promoting any of these would be a **new** decision with its own
  independence accounting; the v8 slot (`c3_v_l65_E5Q`) is already filled.
- Hotspot causality is **not** proven at n=8 per bin per arm. These are exploratory rates.
- No agonism, antagonism or signalling claim is made or implied. These are ECD groove binders.
